I'm a computational fluid dynamics engineer and researcher at the **University of Tabriz**, completing my PhD in
Mechanical Engineering (Energy Conversion). I build and validate simulations of flows where fluid mechanics meets
biology and thermal engineering, and I have taught fluid mechanics and CFD at university level for ten years.

### Research and engineering focus

- **Fluid–structure interaction and biofluid mechanics:** lymphatic valves (doctoral thesis), hemodynamics and respiratory flow
- **Heat transfer:** conjugate heat transfer, heat-transfer enhancement, nanofluids and thermal management, including electronics cooling
- **Numerical methods:** finite-element and finite-volume CFD, lattice Boltzmann methods, verification and validation against experiments

### Tools

`COMSOL Multiphysics` · `Ansys Fluent` · `STAR-CCM+` · `OpenFOAM` · `Python (NumPy, Numba, Matplotlib)` · `MATLAB` · `C++` · `Fortran`

### Featured project

**[Flow Physics in Simulation](https://github.com/HamidrezaToloueinasab/flow-physics-in-simulation)**: an open series of
short, reproducible simulations that each explain one idea in fluid mechanics or heat transfer, validated against
published experiments.

| Nº | Episode | Result |
|:--:|---|---|
| 01 | [When does a wake start to oscillate?](https://github.com/HamidrezaToloueinasab/flow-physics-in-simulation/tree/main/episodes/01-vortex-street) (lattice Boltzmann, Re = 40 vs 100) | St = 0.166 vs 0.164 experiment |
| 02 | [Why isn't blood flow parabolic?](https://github.com/HamidrezaToloueinasab/flow-physics-in-simulation/tree/main/episodes/02-womersley) (pulsatile Womersley flow, α = 1, 5, 15) | 0.003 % vs exact solution |
